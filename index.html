Research Interests

Current directions in manufacturing science and computational materials engineering.

01

Dual-Laser Wire–Powder Additive Manufacturing

Thermal-fluid behavior, auxiliary beam oscillation, wire/powder interaction and multi-material deposition in directed energy deposition.

02

Melt-Pool Dynamics & Material Transport

Keyhole physics, Marangoni convection, melt stirring, element redistribution, mixing and homogenization in metallic melt pools.

03

Microstructure Evolution

Cellular automata, solidification and solid-state phase transformation, crystallographic orientation and grain-growth modeling.

04

Crystal Plasticity & 3D Microstructure

EBSD/DREAM3D reconstruction, HCP crystal plasticity finite-element modeling, slip/twinning and GND-informed deformation analysis.

Selected Projects
