Research Interests

A process–melt pool–transport–microstructure–performance research framework.

01 · Additive Manufacturing

Multi-energy-field Laser Directed Energy Deposition

Laser/plasma hybrid processing, oscillating-laser-assisted wire–powder co-deposition, high-throughput multi-material manufacturing and process-window optimization.

02 · Thermal-fluid Science

Melt Pool Flow and Multi-material Transport

Keyhole behavior, Marangoni convection, melt stirring, material redistribution, mixing and homogenization using high-fidelity CFD and multiphysics simulation.

03 · Microstructure

Solidification and Phase Evolution

Cellular automata, phase transformation, crystallographic orientation evolution, EBSD-based characterization and 3D microstructure reconstruction.

04 · Computational Mechanics

Crystal Plasticity and Tribological Deformation

ABAQUS/UMAT-based CPFEM for HCP metals, slip/twinning, SSD/GND evolution, load transfer and subsurface deformation under frictional contact.
