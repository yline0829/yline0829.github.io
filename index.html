Yulin Ma 马玉林

Ph.D. Candidate, Materials Science and Engineering

School of Materials Science and Engineering, Ocean University of China
Shandong Key Laboratory of Marine Equipment Materials and Protection

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Ocean University of China, supervised by Prof. Hongzhi Cui. My current work focuses on laser directed energy deposition and extreme high-speed laser cladding, with particular interest in melt-pool flow, multi-material transport, microstructure evolution, and their influence on material performance.

Email: auin.m@outlook.com  ·  GitHub: yline0829

Research

Multi-energy-field laser directed energy deposition

Laser/plasma hybrid energy input, oscillating-laser-assisted wire–powder co-deposition, and process-window development for multi-material additive manufacturing.

Melt-pool dynamics and material transport

Experimental and numerical investigation of melt-pool convection, stirring, material redistribution, mixing, and homogenization during deposition.

Solidification and microstructure evolution

Solidification behavior, phase transformation, crystallographic orientation evolution, EBSD analysis, cellular automata, and 3D microstructure reconstruction.

Crystal plasticity and tribological deformation

ABAQUS/UMAT-based crystal plasticity modelling for HCP metals, including slip/twinning, SSD/GND evolution, load transfer, and subsurface deformation under frictional contact.
